Who reads editors best?

Elo-style ratings from Pair Duel. Everyone answers the same question against the same corpus, so the numbers are comparable. You need a display name and 10 duels to appear.

Scores are computed in your browser and sent to the server, which checks them for consistency but cannot verify them. Treat this as a friendly scoreboard.

This game is made for learning what editors seem to like. No comparison should be read as a judgement of relative paper quality.
