Why this exists

The abstract is the front page of your paper, and young researchers are told to produce top-tier work without much explicit practice in spotting what top-tier framing looks like. This game will (maybe) help you train that instinct.

What gets hidden

Authors, institutions, and publication years stay hidden before you choose an abstract, so you cannot draw inferences from author names or paper metadata.

This game is made for learning what editors seem to like. No comparison should be read as a judgement of relative paper quality.
